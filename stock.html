🎙 Add stock

Hold the mic and read the rack: "six lengths of 203 by 133 by 25 UB at twelve metres, rack B"
Speak (or type) what's on the racks, press Parse, check the rows, press Save. The AI only transcribes what you said into rows — every section is then checked against the BAMA steel database: ✓ matched rows get the real kg/m for tonnage; ⚠ unverified rows save fine but carry no weight until corrected. Lengths can be said in metres ("twelve metres") or mm. Several items in one breath is fine — separate with "and" or a pause. Something looks wrong? Mic does nothing → use Chrome/Edge (needs Web Speech) or just type in the box. Section unverified → check the designation matches the steel database format (e.g. 203x133x25 UB, 100x100x8 SHS). Every add, adjust and delete is in the change log with your name — pick it top right.

✂ Cut list

Takes every part on the job's assemblies (length × quantity × assemblies-off, straight from the Tekla imports), groups by section, and packs the cuts into bars — your recorded stock first, then mill lengths — best-fit-decreasing with the kerf added per cut. Each bar shows its cuts and offcut; waste % per section. Deduct used stock takes the consumed bars off the register (audited, restorable via qty). Something looks wrong? A section marked "no stock match" just means nothing on the racks matches — it packs into mill lengths and that's your buying list. Parts with no length (manual BOM rows) are listed but not packed. The plan is a suggestion — it doesn't know a bar is bent or promised elsewhere; check before cutting.
