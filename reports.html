Every cell shows the job number(s) a person clocked to that day, with hours — dates only, no clock times, so you get a feel for output per person per job. Data comes straight from the kiosk (ProjectHours). S000 (non-productive) is hidden unless ticked. Something looks wrong? Someone missing on a day they worked → they didn't clock in, or clocked to the wrong job number. A whole day blank for everyone → check the kiosk was running. Range is capped at 31 days — narrow the dates for longer periods.

This is how much steel each person put through, job by job. Something looks wrong? "Unassigned" rows → the operator wasn't picked on the stage move (it's now required, pre-filled with the last person). 0 kg with pieces → that assembly's drawing carried no weight (manual BOM rows have none). One line per live project. Value = certified AFP value to date (applied where nothing's certified yet, shown in amber). Cost = kiosk labour at basic rates + nett PO commitments. Margin to date = value − cost. Billing position: value earned vs invoiced — positive = under-billed (work done, not yet invoiced = WIP you're funding); negative = over-billed (cash ahead of work). Cash: invoiced, paid, outstanding, retention held. Something looks wrong? Contract £0 → quote lines not seeded (open the project's quote in QB, save once). Value £0 with work done → no AFP raised yet, so the margin column can't see the earning — raise the valuation. Costs are commitments to date, not final: POs lead supplier invoices, labour is basic rate without overheads.

Every unpaid supplier invoice with a due date on or before the chosen date, grouped by supplier. Due dates come from each supplier's payment terms. Include overdue (off by default) adds anything already past its due date — turn it on for a true "what do I owe right now" list. Include direct debits (off by default) adds DD invoices, which the supplier collects automatically — flagged DD so you don't double-pay. Invoices already marked paid (Pay & Remit / BACS run) are excluded. Figures are gross (VAT-inclusive) and live at time of printing. Change a wrong due date by fixing the supplier's payment terms in Office → Suppliers.

Every labour invoice in the period — CIS subcontractors (individuals, deduction retained for HMRC) and labour agencies (limited companies like WPS that supply workers; tag one in Office → Suppliers → payment terms → "Labour supplier"). Materials suppliers are excluded. Gross = amount payable (what leaves the bank). CIS retained = deduction held back from subcontractors for HMRC. Group by supplier to see who you pay; group by week to see the weekly wage-bill shape. Unpaid only shows what's still outstanding. Something looks wrong? WPS still tagged CIS → run the reclassify SQL. A materials supplier appearing → untick "Labour supplier" on their record. Figures are gross and live at time of printing.
